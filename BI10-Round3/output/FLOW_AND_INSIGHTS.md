# Round 2 → Round 3: analysis flow, core insights and top 5 recommendations

Run order: `Code/0. Round2 Customer Scores.py` → existing `1–4` → `Code/5. Customer-to-Shop Growth and Prioritization.py`.

Unit throughout Round 3: **funded contracts over three months, booked at the shop** (window Jul–Sep 2026).

Round 2 validation: CES weights and cut-offs are fit on a random half of customers. **Every outcome reported below comes from the other (hold-out) half.**

## Core insights (the ones everyone will find, proven harder)

1. **Equal sales today does not mean equal room to grow. The difference is how well each shop works the lead pipeline it already has.**
   - Conversion is benchmarked within product, because a CL campaign lead converts at about 2% and a CD point-of-sale lead at about 40%.
   - Half of the assessed shops' headroom sits in 268 shops whose leads convert below the peer rate for the same product.
2. **"Sales recorded at a shop" understates shops that sell cards.**
   - A central ID records 28% of all funded contracts.
   - 9 in 10 cards it records were sold by a physical shop's own staff the same day.
3. **Customer quality from Round 2 holds out of time, but it is a guardrail, not the growth engine.**
   - GROW re-borrows 3–4× more often than the other groups.
   - The R2 Tết campaign is refuted by Round 3 data.

## Limits

- Round 3 holds about 32% of the Round 2 customers' Jan–May 2025 contracts. Outcome rates are therefore "observed in this network", and group comparisons assume the sampling does not depend on the group.
- BAU is the Task 1 trailing baseline. Attainable and ceiling are peer-benchmark scenarios, not causal uplift. The ceiling is an internal proxy, not market size.
- The assessed shops record 21% of network volume. The central IDs and other shops outside the master cannot be peer-benchmarked, so fixing the shop master and card attribution is a prerequisite.
- The FHS ranks Round 3 late payment only weakly (Gini 0.08). Use it as a guardrail, not as a credit decision.
- Late payment means any `dpd_1pd`–`dpd_4pd` flag on loans funded before May 2026; checkpoint timing is undocumented.
