Shop performance & BAU
Four growth numbers, one unit, product-fair benchmarks
Unit: funded contracts recorded at the shop over 3 months · peers: same category × model, ≥ 30 shops with ≥ 15 contracts
Current actual
Funded contracts recorded at the shop, Jul–Sep 2026.
Business-as-usual (BAU)
Task 1 trailing baseline, chosen over a trend model by rolling-origin backtest.
Attainable (p50 / p75 / p90)
BAU + each product's leads converted at the same-product peer rate + GROW customers reactivated at the peer rate.
Internal ceiling
BAU + every lead at the peer p90 rate + every GROW customer. An internal proxy, not market size.
Why product-fairA cash-loan campaign lead converts at ~2%, a point-of-sale (CD) lead at ~40%. Raw conversion would punish shops with many campaign leads.
